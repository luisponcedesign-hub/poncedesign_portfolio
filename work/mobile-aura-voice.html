Seismic  ·  2026

AI-Powered Mobile

A ground-up transformation of Seismic Mobile that embedded Aura AI directly into seller workflows — reversing years of flat engagement and turning the app into an AI-enabled productivity surface.

Client

Seismic

Year

2026

Role

Lead Product Designer

Focus

AI · Mobile · Product UX

The problem

Seismic Mobile had stalled. Monthly active usage had flattened, the information architecture predated the company's move into AI, and sellers who lived in the desktop product had little reason to open the phone app. Meanwhile the market had shifted: buyers expected assistive, conversational tooling everywhere they worked.

The brief was not add AI to mobile. It was to rebuild the mobile surface so that AI had somewhere coherent to live — and so that everything after it could ship faster.

Approach

  • Rearchitected the app shell so navigation, content, and assistive features shared one modern foundation instead of three legacy ones.
  • Embedded Aura Chat into the primary workflow rather than parking it behind a separate tab — the assistant appears where the seller already is.
  • Designed Podcast Generation, turning long-form enablement content into audio a seller can consume between meetings.
  • Ran a Fall release pilot with real customers and folded their feedback into the shipping design.
  • Laid groundwork for what comes next — Aura Voice-to-Voice, Omnipresent Aura Chat, DSR, and Bulk Email on Mobile all build on this architecture.

Results

+25%Mobile MAU year over year, reversing prior stagnation
13,025Monthly active users in Jan 2026, up from 10,395
57Customers enrolled in the Fall release pilot
47/101Shift 2025 survey respondents interested in Mobile with Aura

Customers specifically called out improved usability, navigation, and overall experience. Strategically, the work repositioned Mobile from a checkbox feature into a competitive surface — and the modernized architecture became the scalable foundation the FY26 roadmap depends on.

The redesign didn't just add an assistant. It gave the assistant a product to live in.
