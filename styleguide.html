04 Animation

Motion that explains

Principles

Motion carries meaning or it doesn't ship. Every demo below runs the site's own code; press Replay to run it again.

  • Arrive, don't perform. Things ease out and settle; nothing bounces or loops for attention.
  • Content is never held hostage. Hidden states only apply once html.js is set, and every reveal has a one second fallback.
  • Reduced motion wins. Under prefers-reduced-motion every transition collapses to an instant and decorative canvases draw one still frame.
  • Off screen, off. Canvases and loops stop ticking when they leave the viewport or the tab is hidden.

Headline split

The hero headline breaks into characters that rise in on a 28ms stagger. Words stay whole so lines never break mid word.

Then I build it.
