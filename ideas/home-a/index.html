    About

    MFA Video · SCAD

    Twenty years of making it legible. I design it, then I build it.

    I've worked at SCAD, Razorfish, Viewpoint, WebMD, Vacasa and Seismic. I lead medium-to-large UX projects across web and mobile, run research and testing programs, and mentor the designers around me.

    My MFA is in video, which is why motion keeps showing up in my product work: it's the fastest way to explain a system to the person using it. I write my own HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so what I hand engineering I already know can be built.
