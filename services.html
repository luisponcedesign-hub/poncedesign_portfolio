01 · Product design

Product design for complex software.

I help teams turn tangled enterprise, healthcare and AI products into one experience people understand on the first try.

Who it's for

  • SaaS teams without a senior designer who need someone to own the hard problem, not just the screens.
  • Enterprise product teams with a platform that grew one app at a time and now feels like five products.
  • Healthtech teams designing for people who rely on the product every day.
  • Product leaders with a vague mandate, like “improve the experience,” who need it turned into a roadmap.

Problems I solve

  • Fragmented surfaces: your product lives in several places that don't speak the same language.
  • Too many steps: a common task takes several pages in a legacy admin tool.
  • Content without action: the app shows people information but doesn't help them do anything with it.
  • No shared components: every team builds its own version of the same thing.
  • No way to measure: you can't tell whether the redesign worked.

What you get

  • Journey maps, audits and research synthesis
  • UX strategy and a phased roadmap with success measures
  • Information architecture and navigation
  • Interactive prototypes in Figma
  • Design system components with specs and usage notes
  • Developer handoff and support through build
  • Usability tests with real users

How it works

  1. 01Discover

    I map the jobs people are trying to do against what the product supports today.

  2. 02Decide

    We agree on the few decisions that matter and how we'll know they worked.

  3. 03Design

    I prototype the flows and the components behind them.

  4. 04Validate

    We test with real users before anything ships.

  5. 05Hand off

    I work with your engineers through build, so the design survives contact with code.

02 · AI development

AI features people actually trust.

I design and build AI experiences end to end: the use case, the conversation, the honest line about what the AI can't know, and a working prototype built with Claude Code. The chat on this site is one of them.

Who it's for

  • SaaS teams adding an AI assistant to a product people already use.
  • Teams with a working model and a confusing interface that users don't trust yet.
  • Founders who need a working AI prototype to test with customers or show investors.
  • Design and product teams adopting AI assisted workflows, from Figma to code.

Problems I solve

  • Data locked behind dashboards and exports: people have questions, and the answers sit in CSV files.
  • AI bolted on: a floating AI button that leaves the product's structure unchanged.
  • Unclear limits: users can't tell what the AI knows, so they either over trust it or ignore it.
  • Prototypes stuck in slides: the idea looks good in a deck, but nobody can try it.
  • New surfaces like voice: chat, search, voice and history that need to feel like one assistant.

What you get

  • An AI use case map
  • Conversation design: prompts, suggested questions, empty and error states
  • Honest limits copy about data and accuracy
  • Voice and mobile assistant UX
  • Working prototypes in HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • An AI chat for your own site
  • Workshops on an AI assisted design and build workflow

How it works

  1. 01Find the job

    We pick the questions your users actually ask, not the ones a demo shows off.

  2. 02Design the conversation

    I write the flows, states and limits before any screen.

  3. 03Build it

    I prototype real interactions with Claude Code, so we test the thing, not a picture of it.

  4. 04Test with real questions

    We watch people use it and fix what breaks their trust.

  5. 05Ship or hand off

    I deliver the code, specs and copy your team needs to take it to production.

03 · Video and motion

Motion that explains.

Before product design, I edited video and built motion graphics for Univision, Razorfish and Ensequence, and I hold an MFA in Video from SCAD. Today I use motion to explain products, not to decorate them.

Who it's for

  • SaaS teams launching a feature who need a short video that shows what it does.
  • Product and design teams who need UI motion designed and specified, not guessed at in code.
  • Marketing teams who need one story cut for the site, social and sales decks.
  • Teams with bilingual audiences who need English, Spanish or French versions reviewed by a fluent speaker.

Problems I solve

  • A feature nobody understands from a screenshot: the value only shows when it moves.
  • Motion that feels random: every screen animates differently because nobody set the rules.
  • Raw footage and no time: you have recordings, but no one to shape them into a story.
  • Video features inside your product: recording, playback and editing that grew up one app at a time.

What you get

  • Product explainer and launch videos
  • Storyboards and animatics
  • UI motion specs with durations and easing
  • A motion system: timing tokens, easing, reduced motion rules
  • Edited video from your existing footage
  • Cutdowns for your site and social channels
  • Product UX for video features

How it works

  1. 01Brief

    We agree on the one thing the viewer should understand.

  2. 02Script and storyboard

    You approve the story before anything is animated.

  3. 03Animatic

    A rough cut with timing, so changes stay cheap.

  4. 04Production

    Motion, edit, sound and captions.

  5. 05Delivery

    Final files in every size you need.
